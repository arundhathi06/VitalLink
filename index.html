What VitalLink Does

A smarter way to manage campus donors

Built for college campuses — simple for students, powerful for administrators.

📋
Student Registration
Students register their blood type, contact details, and health status in seconds. Data stored securely in Firebase.
🔒
Secure Admin Panel
Admins log in via Firebase Authentication to access a full dashboard — search, edit, or remove any record.
⏰
Monthly Update Alerts
System tracks last update date and flags donors whose records are overdue for a monthly review.
🚫
Ineligibility Alerts
Automatically flags donors who are temporarily or permanently ineligible based on health conditions or recent donations.
🔍
Smart Search & Filter
Find donors by blood type, name, department, or eligibility status instantly from the admin panel.
📅
Donation History
Track when each donor last donated and calculate eligibility windows automatically.
